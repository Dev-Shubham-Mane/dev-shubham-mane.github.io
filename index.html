Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Your workouts are yours. evryday is designed to keep your workout information on your device. We do not operate a user-data server, sell personal information, or use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Information you choose to store

When you use evryday, information you enter or create may include:

  • Workout routines, exercise selections, workout dates and durations.
  • Set details such as weight, repetitions, completion status, personal records, and notes you add.
  • App preferences and progress information calculated from your workouts.
  • An optional display name and profile photo.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app encrypts its workout database and protects locally stored profile information. We do not receive it unless you choose to export or share it, or enable a backup.

Google sign-in and optional backups

You can use the app without signing in. If you choose Google sign-in, evryday receives basic Google account information such as your email address, name, and profile photo, to display your account and support backup features.

If you set up backups, the app can save your workout database and selected profile information to the app-specific data area of your Google Drive. Before upload, backup contents are encrypted using your backup password or recovery code. We do not receive your password or recovery code and cannot use them to decrypt your backup. Google may process account, file, and connection metadata under its own policies.

Backups are stored in your Google account and may remain there after you sign out of evryday or uninstall the app. Signing out does not delete a backup. You control whether to sign in and use this feature. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Exercise images and network information

Exercise illustrations are loaded from a content delivery network hosted on Cloudflare R2. When an image is requested, your device connects to that service. Like other internet services, the host may receive technical connection information such as your IP address, request time, and browser or device networking headers. Workout logs are not sent with image requests.

Sharing and feedback

Sharing a workout card, report, or CSV is optional. When you choose to share or export, the app sends the selected content to the destination you choose through Android's sharing or file-saving features. The receiving app or service then handles that information under its own privacy policy.

If you send feedback by email, your email provider receives the message. The feedback draft may include the app version and Android version to help us investigate issues; you decide whether to send it and what else to include.

Notifications and diagnostics

Workout reminders and progress notifications are scheduled locally on your device. They are optional and can be disabled in the app or your Android settings. evryday does not send workout notifications from a server.

The app does not send crash reports or analytics to us. In debug builds only, limited error details may be written to the device's local diagnostic log.

Your choices and deleting information

  • Use Settings → Account & Data → Delete All Data to remove workout records stored in the app on this device.
  • Remove a custom profile photo from your profile and manage Google sign-in from the profile screen.
  • Uninstalling evryday removes its local app data. It does not necessarily remove backups already saved to Google Drive or files you exported or shared.

To ask a question about privacy or request help with app-related information, email everyday.workout@gmail.com. Please do not email your backup password or recovery code.

Children's privacy

evryday is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was most recently updated. Material changes will be reflected here.
